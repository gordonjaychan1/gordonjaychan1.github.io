About me

Outside of work I play upright bass, both classical and jazz, and I sing. In university, I sang with the SCU Chamber Singers and played in the orchestra, and I've sung the national anthem at SF Giants and Golden State Warriors games.

Why I do this

Websites

It started with Rika Usami's dojo website. Her old site wasn't the best: the design felt dated and the English translation was rough, and I knew a karate world champion deserved better. So I redesigned it on my own, learned a lot along the way, and pitched it to her when I went to train with her in Japan. I'm lucky she loved it and said yes.

Then I built a site for Camarillo Roofing and realized that for a small business, a website is how they show off everything they've put so much work into and are proud of, the same way I felt about Rika Usami's site. Businesses like roofers and chiropractors take care of their communities. They deserve good-looking websites that show off their strong work, and I'm proud to help build them.

Teaching

I've enjoyed teaching for a long time. I've been lucky to have great teachers in school, in music, and in karate, and they showed me what a good teacher does: help people get the most out of themselves and reach goals they didn't think were possible.

I get really passionate when the people I teach care about reaching their goals, and I want them to be proud of what they do. Tutoring students in academics and coaching the SCU karate team are two ways I get to do that.

Karate

19 years on the mat

I'm a shodan (first-degree black belt) in Hayashi-ha Shitō-ryū, and I've also trained in Shotokan for the last six years. These days I coach the Shotokan Karate team at SCU, and I build things for the karate world, like websites for two dojos and a kata data analysis site.
