FAQ

Common questions

What's included in the monthly fee?

Website hosting, unlimited changes made quickly, keeping your domain connected, and fixing any problems that come up.

What counts as an update, and how quickly do you make changes?

Any change you want is included. Send it to me rather than editing the site yourself. I make small changes within 3 days and bigger ones within 5 days.

How long does it take to build a new site?

I can usually build a website within a week of you asking. It depends on how big a site you want.

Who owns the website?

You own your domain name, business name, logo, and the photos, videos, and business information you give me. I own the website's design and code, including its layout, styling, and site files. As long as our agreement is active and your payments are up to date, you have permission to use the website.

Do you handle the domain name and business email?

I handle the domain name for you. If you already own a domain, you keep paying for it, and I move it over and connect it to your new site. You handle your own business email, so I never see anything confidential.

Do I need to provide the text and photos?

No. I can write the text and find photos online. If you want specific photos used, send them to me and I'll add them. I can add videos too.
